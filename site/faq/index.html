HSAAI Enterprise AI FAQ

What is HSAAI?

HSAAI is a private, sovereign, self-hosted Enterprise AI Platform.

Does HSAAI support Local LLMs?

Yes. The validated path uses the LLM Gateway, Ollama and qwen2.5:7b-instruct.

Does HSAAI support Enterprise RAG?

Yes. It includes Qdrant, hybrid retrieval, Cross-Encoder relevance filtering, grounded answers and citation validation.

Does HSAAI support Arabic AI?

Yes. HSAAI includes Arabic enterprise evaluation scenarios and Arabic enterprise knowledge workflows.

Is HSAAI only a chatbot?

No. It is designed as a broader Enterprise AI platform including identity, security, knowledge, agents, workflows, observability and infrastructure.
