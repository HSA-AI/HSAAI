# Acceptable Use Policy (ISO 27001 A.5.10)

**Document ID:** ISMS-POL-018 | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Date:** 2026-07-05

## 1. Acceptable Use
- HSAAI is for business purposes only
- No personal use of AI services
- No uploading of non-business documents
- No sharing of credentials

## 2. Prohibited Actions
- Attempting to bypass authentication
- Accessing other tenants' data
- Injecting prompts to extract system information
- Running unauthorized tools or scripts
- Downloading sensitive data without approval

## 3. Monitoring
- All activities are logged and monitored
- Users consent to monitoring upon login
- Violations reported to CISO and HR

**Owner:** HR + CISO | **Review:** Annually
