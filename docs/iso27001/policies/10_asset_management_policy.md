# Asset Management Policy (ISO 27001 A.5.9-A.5.11)

**Document ID:** ISMS-POL-010 | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Date:** 2026-07-05

## 1. Asset Inventory
- All hardware, software, data, and services tracked in asset register
- Asset owner assigned for each asset
- Annual asset verification

## 2. Asset Classification
- All assets classified per Data Classification Policy
- Labels applied to digital and physical assets

## 3. Asset Handling
- Media disposal: secure wipe (DoD 5220.22-M) for digital, shredding for physical
- Equipment transfer: requires CISO approval
- Returned equipment: data sanitized before reuse

**Owner:** IT Manager | **Review:** Annually
