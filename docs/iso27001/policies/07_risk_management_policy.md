# Risk Management Policy (ISO 27001 A.5.1-A.5.7)

**Document ID:** ISMS-POL-007 | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Date:** 2026-07-05

## 1. Risk Assessment
- Annual comprehensive risk assessment
- Quarterly risk review
- Risk scoring: Impact (1-5) × Likelihood (1-5) = Risk Score (1-25)
- Risk levels: Low (1-4), Medium (5-9), High (10-15), Critical (16-25)

## 2. Risk Treatment
- Accept: Documented with justification (Low only)
- Mitigate: Implement controls (Medium+)
- Transfer: Insurance or contract (Financial risks)
- Avoid: Discontinue activity (Critical unmitigated)

## 3. Risk Register
- Maintained in docs/iso27001/registers/risk_register.md
- Each risk has: ID, asset, threat, vulnerability, impact, likelihood, score, owner, treatment, residual risk

**Owner:** CISO | **Review:** Quarterly
